# Hatchling Dash 

## Overview

For my project i decided to create a top down runner where you play as a baby turtle hatchling and your objective is to reach the ocean and in order to do so, you must avoid the hungry crabs and seagulls on your way. 

## Obstacles

The game features crab and seagull enemies as you can see and there are also rocks that will block you

## Enemy behavior

The crabs move in between rocks
The seagulls follow and dive until a certain distance to the player

The number of crabs and their speed increases as u get to  higher levels

And the frequency and speed of seagulls increases as levels increase 

## Win/death

When you reach the ocean you advance to the next level

When you get touched by either seagull or crab the game is over and the game restarts from the beginning . I added a little pause when you get touched so that you can gain some feedback


## Sounds

Seagull calls
Crab clicks
Sand shuffling
Ocean waves
Ocean splash

## Highscore/ Score UI

At the top right we have score display to show the current level you are on and at the top left we have your high score which is saved after the game is over


## Turtle mechanics 

No backward movement
Move forward left and right with WASD or arrow keys
